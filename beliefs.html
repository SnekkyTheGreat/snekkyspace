These are some of my beliefs in no particular order. You don't have to interact with me if you don't want to, but you're welcome to even if you disagree with me.

  • I am a member of the Evangelical Presbyterian Church.
    • I believe that Jesus Christ is the only Son of God, fully God and fully human, born of the virgin Mary. I believe that he lived, performed miracles, and was killed via crucifixion to take the punishment for our sins. But because God is more powerful than death, Jesus rose from the dead three days later, and ascended to heaven. I believe that he will come again someday to pass judgement and there will be a new heaven and a new earth.
    • I believe that the Trinity is one God in three persons, the Father, the Son, and the Spirit, all equally one God.
    • I believe that all humans are inherently sinful and totally depraved, and our only hope of salvation comes from Jesus Christ, who we all need desperately. Those who believe in Christ still aren't perfect, but we're counted as perfect because of Christ's sacrifice.
    • We cannot earn our salvation, and the only thing we need to do to get it is to repent of our sins and put our trust fully in Jesus.
    • Predestination or something idk, thinking about it too hard gives me a headache
    • Sharing the gospel politely (e.g. "Jesus loves you," "Do you want to come to church with me?" "I believe x because the Bible says so," "Would you like to talk about Jesus?"), otherwise known as evangelizing, is not forcing religion.
    • For more info, look at the Westminster Shorter Catechism.
  • I do not support LGBTQ+ ideology, but that's just my opinion and I don't try to force it on anyone else. You are welcome to live your life how you want to, I'm not the boss of you. I will still love and respect and support you as a person without agreeing with your worldview. I believe that it is a sin to identify as another gender or to engage in homosexual acts, but these are no worse than any other sin. We all struggle with things, and I don't want to make it harder on you.
  • I do not support therianthropy, but I still love and respect and support those who identify as therians or otherkin. I believe that we're all created in God's image with souls that reflect his glory, and I don't believe in past lives. I believe that it is wrong to identify as an animal, but I'm not going to be rude to you about it. I'm not in charge of you and you are welcome to believe and do whatever you want. Plus masks and tails and quads and stuff are pretty cool.
  • I am firmly pro life.
  • Pineapple DOES belong on pizza.
  • Sleeping with socks on is superior.
  • It is pronounced gif not jif. I will die on this hill. I don't care what the creator of the format says, he's wrong.
  • I don't like swearing. I try not to listen to music that has too much swearing in it, but I'll listen to some.
  • I do not support generative AI in any capacity.
